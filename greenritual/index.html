14-Day Eating Rhythm Reset
Green Ritual Mild Version Your Daily Refuel · 500ml
Ingredients
  • 1–2apples
  • 2–3celery stalks
  • 1–2 inchcucumber
  • 1 handfulspinach
  • ¼avocado
  • as neededwater
Base Recipe

This is a starting point — add or subtract based on your preferences. The most important thing is to massively increase your quantity and variety of green leafy vegetables.

Add Your Twist
Play with these
🍋 lemon juice 🫚 coconut water 🫚 ginger 🥕 carrots 🥦 beetroot 🌿 mint 🍍 pineapple 🥭 mango
How To
  1. 1Use a high-powered blender — NutriBullet or Moulinex work well.
  2. 2Add apples, cucumber and celery first, then the rest.
  3. 3Adjust the amount of water to reach your preferred consistency.
  4. 4Have a minimum of 1–2 glasses each morning.
  5. 5Pre-make your batches and store in the fridge or in single jars for convenience.
  6. 6Adjust sweetness with apples or flavour with spices, coconut water, lemon or ginger.
  7. 7Best served cold. 🌿
Weekend Prep Tip

"Blend your spinach and celery — freeze in ice cube trays. Pre-cut your cucumber and store in the fridge. Monday morning: two cubes, your ingredients, two minutes. Done."

Batch It

Make a large batch and keep in your fridge in an airtight container — it stays fresh for 2 days and is ready whenever you need it.
