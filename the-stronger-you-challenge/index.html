Questions

Questions, answered

Do I need to be fit already?
No. Day 1 meets you exactly where you are, and every exercise is adjusted to your level. Beginners are welcome, and so are women coming back after a long break.
Will I be the weakest one there?
No. You’ll be placed in a small group with women at a similar level to you. You’ll progress at your own pace, it’s not a competition.
Is this one on one?
No. You’ll train in a small group of 3 to 5 women. That’s part of what makes it encouraging. You show up for each other, and the sessions are more fun because of it.
Where do the sessions happen?
Already have a group of friends and a place in mind? I’ll come to you, so training stays close and convenient. New to it or not sure where to go? Train with me at Health & Fitness Gym in Goodlands. We’ll find a day and time that works for your group.
Do I need any equipment?
Everything you need is available at Health & Fitness Gym. Just bring yourself, comfortable clothes and water. If I’m coming to train your group at your location, we’ll just need to make sure the basic equipment is available.
How much time does it take?
Two 50-minute sessions a week, plus whatever small habits you choose to build between them. It's designed to fit a real, busy life, not take it over.
What if I miss a session?
Life happens, and missing a day is not starting over. You keep every material, we adjust, and you pick up where you left off. That is the whole point of building a rhythm instead of a streak.
I've tried things before and stopped. Why would this be different?
This isn't another fitness plan with a diet attached. It's built around how behaviour actually changes and the psychology behind why we eat the way we do. You stop being someone who starts over, and become someone who understands her own body and knows what works for her. That's what makes the habits last long after week 8, instead of running on willpower and discipline alone.
Do I have to count calories or cut out foods?
No calorie counting and no banned foods. You'll learn to understand your own hunger and choices, so eating gets easier, not stricter.
How do I pay, and can I split it?
Once, before we start, by MCB Juice. Message me and I'll send the details and confirm your spot. If you need to split it, you can pay 60% now and 40% at week 4.
